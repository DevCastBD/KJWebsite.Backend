# Data quality (BE-091, #93)

Detection flags and proposes; it never auto-merges, auto-deletes or changes a financial record (donations, postings and receipts stay append-only). Every ruling is a stored, audited decision with who made it and a required note.

## Donation anomalies (FinanceService, `/api/v1/admin/finance/data-quality`)
Rules: `large_amount` (a confirmed donation at or above the larger of the currency's floor, `Finance:DataQuality:LargeFloor`, default BDT 100,000 and USD 1,000 and 10 times the median once 10 donations exist), `rapid_repeat` (the same donor, amount and currency twice within 10 minutes), `confirmed_without_posting` (confirmed, no ledger posting) and `gateway_failure_spike` (at least 10 attempts in 24 hours and at least half failed or cancelled: a gateway problem or a test left running). Thresholds start conservative so the alerts get read; tune them against real data in configuration. A scan runs daily (`ScanIntervalMinutes`, 0 = off) or on `POST .../anomalies/scan` (`finance.reconcile`) and raises each flag once (a unique key), so a reviewed flag stays reviewed. `GET .../anomalies` (`finance.view`; filters `status`, `rule`) lists them; `POST .../anomalies/{id}/review {outcome: confirmed|dismissed, note}` (`finance.donation.approve`) closes one (409 if already reviewed). Review changes nothing but the flag.

## Duplicates
`GET .../duplicates/donors` (Finance, `finance.view`) and `GET /api/v1/admin/membership/data-quality/duplicates/members` (Membership, `membership.manage`) list pairs that may be one person. A shared email (donors) or phone is `high`; the same full name alone is `low`. Evidence says the kind of match (`same_email`, `same_phone`, `same_name`), never the value; the ids lead to the records. A pair is ruled on with `POST .../duplicates/{donors|members}/decisions {leftId, rightId, decision: not_duplicate|confirmed_duplicate, note}` (`finance.donor.manage` / `membership.manage`), after which it leaves the list; a second ruling on the same pair is 409. **A decision changes no record.** Combining two donors or members is a separate step, not built here: donation rows cannot be edited, so a donor merge has to link records rather than rewrite them, and it will act on `confirmed_duplicate` pairs.

## Completeness (MembershipService)
`GET /api/v1/admin/membership/data-quality/completeness` (`membership.manage`): per entity and field, filled, missing, percent and up to 10 ids of records missing it (ids only). Members (not terminated): `nameBn`, `expertise`, `phone`, `address`, `dateOfBirth`, `bloodGroup`, `photo`. Beneficiary institutions (not archived): `nameBn`, `address`, `district`, `mou`, `enrolment`.
