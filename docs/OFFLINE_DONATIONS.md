# Offline donations with maker-checker (BE-058, #63)

Most gifts arrive outside the gateways: bank transfers, cash, cheques, bKash/Nagad sent to a number, goods. They are recorded in the back office under **maker-checker**: one person records, a **different** person approves, and only the approval posts to the ledger. The rule is enforced on the server from the signed-in user ids (`403 SEPARATION_OF_DUTIES`), whatever permissions the user holds.

## Flow
1. **Record** (`finance.donation.record`): `POST /api/v1/admin/finance/donations` (the frontend's path) with `{donorId?, donorName?, email?, phone?, amount, currency, method, date?, designation?, designationId?, anonymous?, dedication?, referenceNo?, note?, valuation?}`. `method` is `bank`, `cash`, `cheque`, `bkash`, `nagad` or `in_kind`; `date` is when it arrived (not in the future, not older than two years); `designation` is `general`, `campaign:slug`, `project:slug` or `student`; `referenceNo` is the bank/cheque/wallet transaction id to match statements. The donation is created **`pending`** with an offline record and posts nothing. Accepts an `Idempotency-Key`. With a `donorId`, the donor's own current name, email and phone are used and typed values are ignored; otherwise donor name is required and email is optional (a walk-in donor may not give one).
2. **Prove** (`finance.donation.record`): `PUT .../donations/{id}/proofs` with the raw file, its `Content-Type` and an optional URL-encoded `X-Filename`. PDF, JPEG, PNG or WebP up to 5 MB, content-checked against the file's own bytes, up to ten per donation, only while awaiting a decision. The SHA-256 and a retention date are stored. `GET .../proofs/{proofId}/file` (`finance.view`) downloads it as an attachment, never cached.
3. **Decide** (`finance.donation.approve`): `POST .../approve` or `POST .../reject {reason}`. Approving confirms the donation and posts it to the ledger in **one commit** with the decision record, dated the day the gift arrived (or today when that month is already closed). A bank, cheque or wallet gift needs at least one proof first (`409 PROOF_REQUIRED`); cash and in-kind do not. Approving twice is harmless; one decision per record (unique in the database). Rejecting cancels the donation with the reason; a mistake is rejected and recorded again, never edited.
4. `GET .../donations/{id}/offline` and the donation detail show the record, proofs, decision and valuation; the donations list shows `method` and `offline: true`.

## Student designation and donor attribution (BE-108, #215)
A student gift requires `donorId: "dnr_..."`, `designation: "student"` (no colon) and `designationId` equal to `stu_` plus 32 hex characters. The request does not accept `student:stu_...`; `designationId` is only allowed with `student`. The student's connection to the donor is the Sponsorship link (#214), never an email match. The recording form can fetch eligible students from Sponsorship; see [SPONSORSHIP.md](SPONSORSHIP.md).

The link must have started on or before the received date and not ended before it, comparing calendar dates. An ended link counts through its end date inclusive; a voided (mistaken) link or one cancelled before it started never counts. This is checked once at recording; approval does not repeat it, even if the link was voided in between. If Sponsorship cannot be reached, recording returns `503 SPONSORSHIP_UNAVAILABLE` and saves nothing.

Invalid fields return `400 VALIDATION_ERROR` through the existing `Invalid fields: ...` message: `designationId` for a missing/malformed student id, an ineligible link, or use with a non-student type; `donorId` for an unknown donor or a missing donor on a student gift; `anonymous` for an anonymous student gift (also without `donorId`, when both fields are named) or an anonymous gift with a donor id. Typed contact details, including malformed email, are ignored with `donorId`.

The gift stores nullable `donations.DonorId`, added without backfilling old rows. Approval takes the receipt's stable `DonorKey` from the donor found by id, so history stays attached even if the donor's email changed after recording. With `donorId`, the gift uses the donor's current name, email, phone and language at recording. Contact details still follow the latest gift whose email is the donor's current email; a gift tied by id with a different email never rewrites the donor. For offline gifts with `donorId`, the receipt snapshot and receipt notification use the donor's current name and email at approval; online gifts keep the typed name and email for the receipt snapshot and notification recipient. Staff correct contact details through the donor profile (#211). A donor id that no longer resolves fails receipt issuing, with no email fallback. Non-anonymous online gifts now store the eligible signed-in donor's id first, otherwise the id matched by current email at recording (#226, BE-112); see [DONORS.md](DONORS.md). Gifts without an id (including anonymous gifts, old rows and walk-ins) retain email matching at confirmation. Old rows and existing receipts are unchanged, with no backfill. Reversal rows still copy `donorId`.

The record response, staff gift rows and gift detail include `donorId` (null when absent), `studentId` and `studentName` (preferred name only). Names come from Sponsorship, are never stored on gifts, and staff reads use one batched lookup per page; an unavailable Sponsorship service leaves the name null with `200`. Maker-checker, proofs and ledger postings are unchanged: a student designation is attribution, not a separate fund, unless fiscal decision #112 says otherwise.

## In-kind gifts
The donation amount is the **valuation**, and the record must carry `valuation: {basis, itemDescription, note?}` with `basis` one of `market_price`, `appraisal`, `donor_receipt`, `cost`, `other` (a note is required for `other`). They post Dr `1250 In-kind gifts received` (asset) / Cr donation income. Handing the item on later is a manual ledger adjustment.

## Retention and evidence
Offline records, decisions and proofs are append-only (application guard and PostgreSQL triggers); no endpoint deletes or edits them. Proof files carry `retainUntil` = upload + `Finance:Proofs:RetentionYears` (default 7, cautious until the retention decision #110). Proof bytes live behind `IProofStore` (local disk at `Finance:Proofs:Path` for now; object storage per ADR-006 later). Every step is audited with its own actor: the donation and record (maker), each proof (uploader), the decision (checker).

## Permissions
`finance.donation.record` and `finance.donation.approve` are held by Admin, SuperAdmin and FinanceOfficer. Further four-eyes controls (adjustments, refunds, reopen) are in CONTROLS.md.
